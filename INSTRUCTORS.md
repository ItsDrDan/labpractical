# Station Rotation: CHEM 3A Lab Practical (instructor notes)

Practice for the CHEM 3A lab practical, matched to the **Fall 2026 CHEM 3A Lab Manual** and its Lab Practical Study Guide. It's free for FCC students and runs in any browser or inside Canvas.

**Play:** https://itsdrdan.github.io/labpractical/
**Set it up for your class:** https://itsdrdan.github.io/labpractical/setup.html

## Get a link for your class (2 minutes)

1. Open the setup page and pick your name. Professors with a public Simple Syllabus syllabus get their labs checked automatically.
2. Check or uncheck labs to match what your section actually ran.
3. Copy the link (or the iframe code) into a Canvas page or module. Students who open it see only your labs. Your name also appears in the game's feedback form.

If you change your lab list later, make a new link. Students who open the new link get the new list.

## What students do

- **Mock Practical:** 10 timed stations (4 minutes + 1 to rotate, then a 5-minute wrap-up), like the real practical: 8 lab stations plus 2 general stations (equipment names and measurements). Every station shows Versions A, B and C. The student picks a green, yellow or purple sheet and answers only their version. Answers are typed and graded like the practical: value, sig figs or decimal places, and units. Each station is worth 10 points.
- **Practice by Lab:** untimed questions for each lab, with hints, worked answers, feedback on common mistakes and a matching video.
- **Lab Bench:** PhET simulations and hands-on virtual benches (reading glassware, weighing, density, titration).

Beginner level shows answer choices. Intermediate and above are typed, with no multiple choice, the same as the real practical.

## Good to know

- **Reaction types:** Types of Reactions asks for **Scheme 2** (precipitation, acid/base neutralization, gas evolution, redox, combustion, no reaction). A Scheme 1 answer gets a note telling the student which scheme is being asked.
- **Older labs:** a few questions come from earlier manuals and are labeled "(earlier manual)": the 9a standard curve and the specific heat of a metal. They stay out of the mock practical. Exp 3z (Found or Forgery) is only on Dr. Dan's list.
- **Readings:** the mock accepts readings within the practical's usual window (about ±0.1 mL on a buret, ±0.3 mL on a 50/100 mL cylinder, ±0.3 °C on a thermometer). Students must still record the right number of decimal places.
- **Privacy:** progress stays on the student's device. The game sends only anonymous practice stats (topic and right or wrong; no names, IDs or typed answers). Browsers set to Do Not Track send nothing.

## Feedback

Students can rate the game and leave comments from inside it, and they pick their professor there. Please send corrections or requests to Dr. Dan (ItsDrDan). Something on your practical that isn't covered is exactly what's worth hearing about.
